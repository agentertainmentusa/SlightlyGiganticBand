Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026

Introduction

Slightly Gigantic, operated by A.G. Entertainment, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through slightlygiganticband.com, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

Contact form

When you contact us through the Site, we may collect your name, email address, and the content of your message.

Text Club

When you join the Slightly Gigantic Text Club, we collect your phone number and first name. You may also choose to provide your last name, email address, and state.

Website analytics

We may collect anonymized information about page views and referral sources to understand how visitors use the Site. This information does not include personally identifiable information.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and requests;
  • To send promotional SMS messages, when you have opted in, about shows, tour dates, new music, and related offers;
  • To improve the Site and its content; and
  • To communicate important updates about the Band, the Site, or services you requested.

SMS-Specific Privacy Terms

  • We collect phone numbers only when you voluntarily opt in to receive messages.
  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes at any time.
  • Text messages are sent through EZ Texting, which is contractually bound to protect your data.
  • You may opt out at any time by texting STOP to any of our messages.

How We Share Information

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We share information only with service providers that help us operate the Site and related services, including EZ Texting, Supabase, Vercel, and Formspree, and only as needed for those purposes.
  • We may disclose information when required by law, including in response to a valid court order or subpoena.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to carry out the purposes described in this Policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or until you request deletion where applicable.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing tony@agentertainmentusa.com. California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and residents of the European Union may have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Children’s Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided us personal information, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

Cookies

The Site uses minimal cookies, including session cookies for the admin panel. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking cookies.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-Party Links

The Site may link to external services, including Instagram, Spotify, and other platforms. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Your continued use of the Site after changes are posted is subject to the updated Policy.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email tony@agentertainmentusa.com.
